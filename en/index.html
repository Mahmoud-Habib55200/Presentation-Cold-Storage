FAQ

Common questions about cold storage management software

If your question is not answered here, send it over and we will answer from real implementation experience.

What exactly is a cold storage management system?
A cold storage WMS digitises every operation in a refrigerated warehouse: receiving shipments, assigning a storage slot to each pallet, tracking temperature, ordering dispatch by expiry, and invoicing. What separates it from an ordinary warehouse system is that it treats temperature and shelf life as first-class data, not optional fields — which is what prevents spoilage and keeps every movement documented.
What is FEFO and why does it matter in cold storage?
FEFO stands for First Expired, First Out — the stock closest to expiry leaves first, rather than the oldest received as under FIFO. For refrigerated goods this is decisive: a shipment received late may well expire first. The system orders dispatch automatically by expiry date and blocks the wrong pick, which cuts measurable spoilage within the first months of operation.
How does the system track cold room temperature?
Every chamber is assigned a target temperature and an allowed range. The dashboard shows the current reading per chamber in real time and raises an immediate alert when it drifts out of range. Every reading is written to a historical log you can pull up for a review, an audit, or a dispute with a customer over the condition of their goods.
Does it handle more than one warehouse or site?
Yes. You can run an unlimited number of warehouses and chambers from the same account, each with its own map, temperatures and permissions. Management sees every site on one screen, while each warehouse supervisor sees only their own. Transfers between chambers or between sites are recorded as full movements with user and timestamp.
Do I need special hardware to run it?
No. The system runs in the browser on any computer, and the driver and handling tablet app works on a standard tablet. For scanning you can use the tablet camera or an ordinary barcode reader (USB or Bluetooth). If you already have temperature sensors installed, we can connect them to the system instead of entering readings manually.
Does it include billing for storage customers?
Yes, if you rent refrigerated space to third parties. Each customer has a file with their contract and agreed rates, and every pallet stored in their name. The system calculates the invoice automatically from storage duration, pallet count and any additional services, and tracks paid versus outstanding so no invoice slips past you.
What happens to the warehouse data I already have?
We start with a setup session where we recreate your real warehouse layout inside the system (chambers, aisles, racks), then import your current stock from Excel files or your previous system. Once imported, a barcode is printed for every existing pallet and linked to its position on the map, so you are operational from day one.
How long do implementation and training take?
It depends on warehouse size and the number of SKUs, but a typical single-warehouse implementation takes two to four weeks, including layout setup, stock import and team training. Training runs by role — management, warehouse supervisor, accountant and drivers — and technical support continues after go-live.
Is the system available in Arabic?
Yes. The Arabic interface was built natively with full RTL support, not translated from a foreign product. That shows in the terminology, in the direction of screens and reports, and in how easily warehouse crews use the tablet app without a language barrier.
